Congratulations to [Julie McDonald]({{ "/members/julie-mcdonald/" | relative_url }}), who has been awarded a Rockefeller [Women & Science](https://www.rockefeller.edu/support-our-science/women-and-science/about/) Postdoctoral Fellowship.
Julie will join the lab as a postdoctoral fellow in February 2026.

Julie is a biochemist focused on the interactions between microbes and their environments.
In her doctoral research at MIT, Julie developed directed evolution technologies to improve biological carbon fixation.
