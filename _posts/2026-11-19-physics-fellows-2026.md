title: "Now recruiting: Physics Fellows at Rockefeller"
tags: news, announcement
---

Rockefeller's [Center for Studies in Physics and Biology](https://cspbfellow.rockefeller.edu/) is recruiting postdoctoral fellows this year.

The Physics Fellows program is an independent fellowship with excellent benefits and a strong track record.
The Center is looking for physicists, mathematicians and computer scientists with a demonstrated interest in the interface between their discipline and biology.
Applications are due **November 30**.
Please share this with mentees and colleagues who might be a good fit!
