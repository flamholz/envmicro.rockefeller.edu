[Julie McDonald]({{ "/members/julie-mcdonald/" | relative_url }}) has joined the lab as a postdoctoral fellow, supported by a Rockefeller [Women & Science](https://www.rockefeller.edu/support-our-science/women-and-science/about/) Postdoctoral Fellowship, which Julie was awarded in November 2025.

Julie is a biochemist focused on the interactions between microbes and their environments.
Her doctoral research in the [Shoulders group](https://shoulderslab.mit.edu/) at MIT developed directed evolution technologies to improve biological carbon fixation.
Welcome, Julie!
