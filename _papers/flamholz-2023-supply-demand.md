---
title: Matching metabolic supply to demand optimizes microbial growth
doi: 10.1016/j.tim.2023.06.003
description: Microbes are thought to allocate their biosynthetic capacity to maximize growth, yet many grow faster after laboratory evolution. A comment on a model that resolves the conundrum.
---

Recent research has strengthened the notion that microbes allocate their biosynthetic capacity to maximize the growth rate. Yet many microbes can grow substantially faster after laboratory evolution. Here [Akshit Goyal](https://www.icts.res.in/people/akshit-goyal) and Avi Flamholz comment on a resource-allocation model from [Chure and Cremer](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.84878), derived from first principles, that offers a resolution to this conundrum.
