---
title: eQuilibrator — the biochemical thermodynamics calculator
doi: 10.1093/nar/gkr874
description: A free web tool that pairs a database of biochemical thermodynamic data with simple search, so anyone can compute reaction energies under realistic conditions.
---

The laws of thermodynamics constrain how biochemical systems work, but thermodynamic data on biochemical compounds can be hard to find and cumbersome to calculate with. Even a simple question like "how much Gibbs energy is released by ATP hydrolysis at pH 5?" is made needlessly complicated by the search for accurate data.

[eQuilibrator](https://equilibrator.weizmann.ac.il/) addresses this by coupling a comprehensive database of thermodynamic properties of biochemical compounds and reactions with a simple online search and calculation interface. Users can compute Gibbs energies of compounds and reactions at any pH, ionic strength, and metabolite concentrations. The code is open source and the underlying data are freely downloadable. A close collaboration with [Elad Noor](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=E4UymDMAAAAJ&hl=en), with Arren Bar-Even and Ron Milo. eQuilibrator remains in active development and wide use; see the [Resources]({{ "/resources/" | relative_url }}) page.
