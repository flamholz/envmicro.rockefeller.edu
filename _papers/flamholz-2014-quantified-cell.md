A prelude to [*Cell Biology by the Numbers*](http://book.bionumbers.org/) on the importance of attaching numbers to biological phenomena, for without them we are just guessing wildly. We use the example of actin-based motility to show how quantitative reasoning can link diverse studies together and support inferences about cell physiology. We argue that all hypotheses should be quantified, because the process forces you to confront what you do not know and what you cannot reasonably approximate. With Rob Phillips and Ron Milo.
