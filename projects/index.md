# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-wrench" %}Projects

Some projects in the group include:

- Understanding and engineering microbes that eat CO₂ (e.g., [Flamholz et al. PNAS 2022](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2210539119), [eLife 2020](https://elifesciences.org/articles/59882))
- Estimating environmental chemistry from DNA sequences (e.g., [Flamholz et al. mSystems 2024](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/msystems.00763-24))
- Collective CO₂ production by microbial communities (e.g., [Goyal et al. PNAS 2023](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2309387120))
- Fundamentals of microbial physiology and metabolism (e.g., [Flamholz et al. PNAS 2025](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2404048121))
- Environmental analysis of human choices (e.g., diet) and proposed biotechnologies (e.g., [Eshel et al. PNAS 2025](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2404329122), [Lovat et al. Nat. Biotechnol. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-025-02632-w))

Skim a paper or two.
You'll see that we integrate experimental, computational, and theoretical tools to address questions that are mostly focused on microbial metabolism.
