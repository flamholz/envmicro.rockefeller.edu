Avi is an Assistant Professor at The Rockefeller University and head of the Laboratory of Environmental Microbiology.
In work time, Avi is often thinking about how physical laws and economic principles affect the behavior and organization of cells, organisms and ecosystems.

Avi did postdoctoral research with [Dianne Newman](https://dknweb.caltech.edu/), [Rob Phillips](https://www.rpgroup.caltech.edu/) and [Woody Fischer](https://web.gps.caltech.edu/~wfischer/) at Caltech, and a Ph.D. with [David Savage](https://www.savagelab.org/) at UC Berkeley.
Before Berkeley, Avi worked in the [Milo Lab](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/plants/milo/) at the Weizmann Institute of Science, at Via Transportation and at Google NYC.
People sometimes assume Avi is Israeli because of the name and the Weizmann affiliation, but New York City has been home to the family for three generations.

Avi is a faculty member in the [David Rockefeller Graduate Program](https://www.rockefeller.edu/education-and-training/graduate-program-in-bioscience/) and the [Tri-Institutional M.D.-Ph.D. Program](https://www.rockefeller.edu/education-and-training/tri-institutional-md-phd-program/).
See Avi's [CV](https://flamholz.github.io/assets/AFlamholz_CV_Long.pdf) for more.
