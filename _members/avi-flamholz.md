Avi is Assistant Professor and head of the Laboratory of Environmental Microbiology at The Rockefeller University (2025–).
He combines genomics, synthetic biology, and computational science to understand the genetics that underlie microbial nutrient processing, and to model how the dynamics of microbial communities contribute to the global carbon cycle and climate.

Avi earned a B.S.E. in computer science from Princeton University (2007) and a Ph.D. in molecular and cell biology from the University of California, Berkeley (2019).
He was a postdoc at the California Institute of Technology (2020–2024), where he built computational tools to model the coupling of microbial growth and respiration in soil-like environments.
His awards include an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship (2014), a Jane Coffin Childs Memorial Postdoctoral Fellowship (2021), and a Burroughs Wellcome Career Award at the Scientific Interface (2023).

Avi is a faculty member in the [David Rockefeller Graduate Program](https://www.rockefeller.edu/education-and-training/graduate-program-in-bioscience/) and the [Tri-Institutional M.D.-Ph.D. Program](https://www.rockefeller.edu/education-and-training/tri-institutional-md-phd-program/).
