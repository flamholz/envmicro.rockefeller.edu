Javi is a microbiologist and metabolic engineer working at the interface of synthetic biology and redox metabolism.
In his doctoral research in the [Nikel group](https://www.biosustain.dtu.dk/research/research-areas/sustainable-chemicals/Systems-Environmental-Microbiology) (Technical University of Denmark, DTU Biosustain) he studied redox cofactors and synthetic one-carbon assimilation in microbes.
